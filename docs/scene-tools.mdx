# Scene behavior tools

These tools can change what appears beyond the direct camera view. Review the entire route and any secondary effects before keeping a result.

| Tool | Key review |
| --- | --- |
| **Generated PCG scheduling** | Confirm source systems, regeneration, gameplay events, and generated appearance. A local change may be overwritten by the source graph. |
| **Static scene captures** | Check recurring captures and the screens, reflections, or materials that use them during playback. |
| **WPO distance limits** | Protect VAT, wind, and required movement; inspect deformation and shadows across the cutoff. |
| **Ray tracing visibility** | Inspect reflections, refraction, indirect light, and shadows, as well as the main view. |
| **Far/small instance cull range** | Watch the full route for popping, missing silhouette, animation, or shadow contributions. |
| **Far contributor visibility trial** | Check projected-size and distance evidence, then inspect lighting and reflections across the scene. |

Being outside the camera does not mean an object contributes nothing to the image. Use [camera coverage and protection](../audit-and-coverage/) to understand what the recorded route covers.
