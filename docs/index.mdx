Optimize Bar helps you examine a shot, prepare possible changes, apply selected changes, and judge the result. Its **ICVFX / Virtual Production**, **Cinematic Rendering**, and **Game** modes can show different controls and objectives. Choose the mode that matches your work before setting up the camera and scene scope.

:::caution Before changing a scene
Work from a recoverable project copy. Preparing a plan only gathers evidence; **Run Selected Plan** applies the reviewed changes. An Editor preview is useful evidence, but it does not certify a final nDisplay, packaged-game, or Movie Render Queue result.
:::

## Words used throughout this guide

| Term | Plain meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Audit | A capture of diagnostic views, performance data, and logs for a chosen scene and shot. |
| Director | The mode's control for choosing the camera and shot route to review. |
| Level Sequence | Unreal's timeline for a shot or sequence. |
| Coverage | The camera route and visible scope recorded for review. It is not a speed measurement. |
| Plan | A list of possible optimization steps, with evidence and eligibility for each target. |
| Target evidence | The detailed list of what a step can affect and why other items were excluded. |
| Journal | The record used to understand an applied batch and support guarded recovery. |
