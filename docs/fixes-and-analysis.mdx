# Fixes, Analysis, and standalone actions

**Fixes** and **Analysis** have their own findings and selection controls. Open the proposed action, inspect affected scope, and read the target evidence. A read-only analysis is not an applied fix.

<img className="docImage" src={fixesImage} alt="Optimize Bar Fixes panel and findings controls" />
<p className="docCaption">Fixes and findings panel from the 1.0.0 guide.</p>

Standalone Scene tools may be used separately where supported, including manual selection workflows. Their controls and prerequisites can differ from Planner steps. Check the exact tool status and selected targets before running an action, then verify the result independently.

The Planner also presents reviewed Scene services for **Nanite** and **mesh-first ISM**, alongside scanned Fixes and Analysis actions. Scan or prepare, inspect the asset or actor list and exclusions, choose eligible entries, authorize, and execute. For ISM, inspect source groups and journaled recovery. For Nanite, review all asset consumers, resource builds, and appearance.
